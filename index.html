04 / Good to know

A little preparation.
A better trip.

↟

Muir Woods parking

Target a 9:00 AM entry on Sunday, Nov 29. Parking reservations are required and separate from park admission. This is a target time, not a reserved slot.

Download the parking confirmation and offline map before leaving; cell service is limited. Eat lunch afterward in Sausalito—picnicking is not permitted in the monument.

Check parking availability ↗
❄

Carry chains. Watch conditions.

Use Highway 140 / El Portal Road. Chain controls are less frequent here than on 41 or 120, but can apply before the hotel and inside the Valley. Tioga and Glacier Point Roads are excluded from this plan.

For the RWD Tesla, R2 controls require traction devices on both rear wheels. Carrying them does not mean using them on dry roads. Drive manually with chains; don’t rely on FSD for snow or ice.

Official chain requirements ↗
ϟ

Charge with breathing room

Start with a comfortable charge. Let Tesla navigation plan stops via Merced and Mariposa; allow a charging and lunch buffer on travel days. Yosemite View Lodge has Tesla Superchargers.

Before entering the Valley, leave enough range for the return, cold weather and delays. Check charger availability in the car; don’t assume free charging.

El Portal charging location ↗

A useful purchase for future trips?

Yes, if the tire fit stays the same.

The König CG-9 102 kit is Tesla’s approved choice for compatible factory 18-inch Model 3 wheels and tires. Tesla lists size 103 for 19-inch wheels. Fit depends on the full tire size and vehicle clearance, not wheel diameter alone.

The same pair can be reused while it remains undamaged and fits the vehicle. Rinse off road salt, dry fully, and inspect before each use. No jack is needed; practice fitting and removing both rear chains at home. Remove wheel covers first.

Friday’s bus alternative—and the Thanksgiving exception

YARTS serves Yosemite View Lodge. Friday’s published winter timetable: 9:42 AM hotel → 10:20 AM Curry Village; return 4:40 PM Curry Village or 5:00 PM Yosemite Valley Lodge → 5:30 PM hotel. Use the Friday “Bus day” option above to see a revised itinerary.
